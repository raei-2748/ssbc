About SSBC

A shared stage for Sydney’s next generation of business thinkers.

SSBC is a student-led council of school business and finance societies across Sydney. We run an entry-level interschool case competition and build the learning and community around it.

Our mission

Connecting students with the realities of business and finance.

To connect NSW secondary students with the practical realities of business and finance through credible, industry-informed experiences that develop strategic judgement, financial literacy and responsible decision-making.

Why we exist

Entry-level finance competitions barely exist.

Most business and finance contests for school students are either too advanced for beginners or simply not on offer. We think every student should be able to try making a real business decision, whether or not they have a finance background.

We also want the council to reflect the whole of Sydney, with a mix of schools, genders and backgrounds, rather than a handful of the best-known names.

Where we came from

SSBC grew out of a business competition between The King’s School and Sydney Grammar School that had been paused for about three years. Student leaders at several schools revived it and opened it up to every NSW school.

Governance

Student-led, with adults still being appointed.

SSBC is run by students and supported by supervising teachers at participating schools. We have no adult committee yet.

We are forming one (a chair, treasurer and public officer, plus other adults) to take on governance, banking, insurance, compliance and safeguarding, with the aim of becoming an incorporated association. Until then, partners and schools should treat SSBC as a new student initiative, and we will say so plainly.
