Beyond the competition

Two ways to grow, whether or not your team is competing this term.

SSBC Learn gives every student a fast, self-paced way into business fundamentals. SSBC Network keeps member schools coordinated year-round — not just during competition season.

SSBC Learn

Short, self-paced modules students can finish between classes — no textbook, no prerequisite. Each one stands alone, so students can start wherever is most useful.

Module 1

How a business makes money

Revenue, costs, and margin — the three numbers behind every business decision, explained with a real small-business example.

≈ 12 minutes
Module 2

Reading an income statement

What revenue, expenses, and net profit actually tell you — and the two questions to ask before trusting a number.

≈ 15 minutes
Module 3

Debt versus equity

Two ways to fund growth, and why the choice between them shapes every decision a company makes afterward.

≈ 14 minutes
Module 4

Market research fundamentals

How to test whether an idea has demand before spending a dollar building it.

≈ 10 minutes
Module 5

Valuation, roughly

A back-of-envelope way to estimate what a company might be worth, and why analysts rarely agree on it.

≈ 16 minutes
Module 6

Pitching a decision

Structuring a recommendation so a busy judge — or a busy investor — understands it in the first thirty seconds.

≈ 11 minutes

Council structure

SSBC Network

Each member school sends one or two student delegates to the council. Delegates meet each term to coordinate shared events, speaker sessions, and opportunities that no single school's society could run alone.

The network exists year-round — it's how member schools stay connected between competition seasons, and how new schools get folded into the council.

Delegates
One to two students per member school, elected by their own society.
Termly meetups
Council-wide sessions to plan events and share what's working.
Shared opportunities board
Speaker sessions, mentoring, and internship leads posted across the whole council.

Bring SSBC Learn and Network to your society

Both programs are free for member schools and run alongside whatever your business society already does.
