Competition rules

Fair, open and judged on the quality of the work.

A summary of how the 2027 competition is run. The full participant handbook will be published before registration opens.

Eligibility

Who can enter and how teams are made up.

Schools

Member societies

Entry is through a school’s business, finance or investment society. A staff supervisor confirms the school’s entry and results.

Teams

Three to four students

Teams are put together by each society. We propose a cap of three teams per school, to be confirmed in the handbook.

Access

Open preliminary round

Round 1 is open to all eligible schools, and finalists are chosen on merit rather than school reputation.

How we keep it fair

The same rules apply to every team.

Do all teams get the same information?

Yes. Every team receives the same controlled case materials through scheduled release windows, with fixed deadlines and clear rules on collaboration, late submissions and outside help. Release windows may be staggered so faster teams cannot pass on their answers.

Can we use AI?

Limited use for research or editing is allowed, but it has to be disclosed. Each team submits an AI-use declaration and lists its key sources and assumptions. Outsourcing the substantive analysis to AI or to another person is not allowed.

How are submissions judged?

Submissions are coded so markers do not see school names, then assessed against one published rubric. Judges declare conflicts of interest, scores are moderated where needed, and teams near the finalist cutoff are double-marked.

How do you check the work is the team’s own?

Finalists present live and answer questions, so judges can check that teams understand and can defend what was submitted in their names. We also spot-check the sources teams cite.

What if there is a technical problem?

Submissions are confirmed on receipt, and a backup form or email is available. Deadline extensions are possible for verified technical failures.

Can we appeal a result?

Appeals are limited to procedural errors or breaches of the published rules, not to disagreements with a judge’s opinion.

Is it accessible?

No paid databases or subscriptions are needed. Schools can ask for reasonable adjustments for students who need them.

Can sponsors influence the outcome?

No. Partner support never affects judging, which teams qualify, or access to the competition.

Questions about the rules?

Email us and we will answer, then add it to the handbook.
