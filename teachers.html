For teachers and supervisors

Everything you need to approve an entry.

What we ask of your school, what we handle, and how much time it takes.

What your school provides

1

A staff supervisor

One teacher who liaises with SSBC and confirms the school’s entry and results.

2

A little time from each team

Each episode is designed to take roughly 30–45 minutes, and the whole of Round 1 about two to three hours. Schools choose whether it runs in or outside class time.

3

A society to enter through

Teams of three to four students are formed by your business, finance or investment society.

What SSBC provides

Materials

Everything teams need

A short primer on how a company makes money and an ungraded practice episode, so no finance background is assumed.

Fairness

Published rules and rubric

Anonymous marking, an AI-use policy and a clear appeals process. See the rules.

Students

Privacy and consent

Consent is sought before student names, photos or recordings are used. See privacy & safeguarding.

How to register

Step 1: Email us

Send your school and society name to hello@ssbcouncil.org, or use the form on the Get involved page.

Step 2: Short call

We schedule a call with your staff supervisor to answer questions and confirm the details.

Step 3: Register teams

Register your teams before the Term 1, 2027 launch. See the proposed timeline.

Is there a cost?

Entry costs are kept low and confirmed at registration.

Ready to enter?

Start with one email and we will take it from there.
