02 / THE 2027 COMPETITION

Four episodes.
One company.
Your decisions.

Teams of three to four students take over a fictional Australian infrastructure operator and steer it through four live episodes — no finance background assumed, no single right answer. Every team receives the same information at the same time.

Round 1 runs entirely online. Finalists present in person to a professional judging panel at the live final.

See the full competition guide
  1. 01
    Growth
    Fund and price an expansion into a live infrastructure market.
  2. 02
    Competition
    A rival operator enters, and the team has to respond.
  3. 03
    Shock
    An energy or regulatory shock tests the plan under pressure.
  4. 04
    Financing
    A debt-versus-equity call, closed out with a recorded pitch.
