Three programs, one council

Each existing school business society keeps its own identity. SSBC is the shared layer that connects them — a competition to compete in, a curriculum to learn from, and a network to belong to.

Compete

SSBC Competition

An interschool business case competition, opening with an online qualifying round and closing with a live final judged by industry professionals.

Learn

SSBC Learn

Short, self-paced resources students can finish in 10–20 minutes — how a business makes money, reading an income statement, debt versus equity, and market research fundamentals.

Connect

SSBC Network

A council of member societies, each represented by one or two student delegates, coordinating events, speakers, and opportunities across schools.

2027 Sydney Interschool Business Case Competition

A company that unfolds in real time, not a report due in three weeks.

Teams of three to four students take over a fictional Australian infrastructure operator and steer it through four live episodes — no finance background assumed, no single right answer. Every team receives the same information at the same time.

Round 1 runs entirely online. Finalists present in person to a professional judging panel at the live final.
