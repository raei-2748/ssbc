Privacy & safeguarding

Students’ information is handled carefully.

A plain-language summary of how we treat personal information. It will be updated as SSBC adopts formal policies with its adult committee.

What we collect and why

This website

The site does not set cookies or run analytics. It loads its font from Google Fonts, and our hosting providers (Cloudflare and GitHub) may keep standard server logs. The enquiry form on Get involved only prepares an email on your device. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Enquiries by email

If you email us we use your name, school or organisation and message only to reply and to route your enquiry to the right person on the council.

The competition

We will collect team member names, school, submissions and recorded pitches to run and judge the competition. Access to student data is limited to the people who need it, and we will set out how long files are kept and when they are deleted in the participant handbook.

Photos, names and recordings

We obtain appropriate consent before using student names, photographs, recordings or school information in promotional or post-event material.

Safeguarding

Who is responsible?

Each participating school keeps its own supervision and child-safety obligations. SSBC works with a supervising staff member and is forming an adult committee that will oversee safeguarding and compliance.

Do you have a child-safety policy?

A formal policy is being developed with the adult committee and will be published here. We would rather say that than imply one already exists.

Who do I contact?

Email hello@ssbcouncil.org with any privacy or safeguarding question, or to ask us to correct or remove information about you or your student.
