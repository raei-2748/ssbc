Get involved

SSBC is built by its member schools and grows through partnership.

Whether you're a teacher, a professional, or a student — here's exactly where to start and what happens next.

Schools

Bring your school's business, finance, or investment society into the council and register teams for the 2027 competition.

  1. 1 Email us with your school and society name
  2. 2 We schedule a short call with your staff supervisor
  3. 3 Register teams ahead of the Term 1, 2027 launch
Register your school →

Sponsors & partners

Support case development, provide judges or mentors, or help fund delivery of a multi-school competition and final.

  1. 1 Tell us which partnership tier interests you
  2. 2 We'll send tier details and next steps
  3. 3 Confirm involvement ahead of the live final
Start a partnership →

Students

Not part of a member society yet? Reach out — we'll connect you with your nearest school representative.

  1. 1 Tell us your school and year group
  2. 2 We connect you with your school's delegate
  3. 3 Join a team or start a society if one doesn't exist
Get in touch →
